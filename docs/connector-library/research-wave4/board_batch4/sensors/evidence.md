# SparkFun Qwiic breakout geometry evidence

## Charter
Goal: validate 12 disjoint exact SparkFun sensor/I/O SKUs for physical contacts, pitch, rows and shipped population. Scope: Qwiic breakouts. Object: exact SKU and PCB geometry. Subject: manufacturer Eagle elements and product imagery. Type: descriptive; working knowledge from previous CAD extraction. Questions: full PTH rows, auxiliary cable/terminal connectors, assembly state and revision scope.

## Canonical sources
1. Official product pages, product JSON-LD SKU metadata, and linked SparkFun hardware repositories.
2. Manufacturer Eagle PCB files pinned to commit, including the explicit v1.1 branch for BOB-16784.
3. Direct inspection of official product photographs for bare-hole vs fitted-connector population.

## Scope filter
No display, relay or cable product was admitted. Candidate CAB-17124 was an unrelated cable and discarded; Qwiic Relay COM-15093 was left outside this sensor/IO batch. No electrical-current or JIG-fit claims are inferred. SMT pads, mounting holes, anchors and solder jumpers do not count as header contacts.

## Object filter
CAD single-pin and short-row elements merged only when transformed pad-center coordinates form one uninterrupted 2.54 mm row; scripts assert this condition. Examples: TMP117 4+1 becomes 5, MicroPressure 4+2 becomes 6, and VL53L1X 4+1+1 becomes 6.
Qwiic Mux BOB-16784 uses v1.1 with pass-through port: ten Qwiic ports and one reset pad, not a conventional edge-comb row. GPIO screwless terminals and ADC screw terminal are classified separately from PTH headers. Qwiic Scale spring terminal has four wire positions but eight solder legs; the separate seven-position bare internal row is auxiliary.
All product populations were inspected in saved images. Qwiic Scale additional images 02/03/04 show both the 6-contact edge row and 7-contact internal row clearly.

## Conclusions
F1–F12 support 12 exact-SKU records, 15 PTH groups and 84 total PTH group contacts. Of these, the 7-contact Scale internal row and the 3-contact ToF Imager auxiliary control row have explicit auxiliary roles. The ten-position ADS1015 header is two rows, not a ten-long single row.
Two additional singleton control pads are outside headers: Mux J21 and GPIO J7. Qwiic ports and fitted terminals are independently recorded in auxiliary_connectors. All multi-contact pin-header footprints are bare/unpopulated in the observed factory product images.

## Next steps / Risks
Fit remains unknown for every record. Board photos represent manufacturer stock, not a delivered individual unit. CAD revisions are pinned; later manufacturing changes require rechecking. Terminal footprints and Qwiic cable ports must not enter ordinary 2.54 mm comb proposals. Interior rows may have component or connector-body clearance limitations.
Single control pads use null pitch because a one-contact group has no within-group spacing. Sensor function, electrical compatibility, retention, contact resistance and current performance are not qualified by this record.

## Validation
Self-review read manufacturer-linked CAD and compared population to official photos. CAD extraction includes individual pad positions and net names. All 12 IDs were selected outside earlier Adafruit/Pololu batches and the initial SparkFun SEN-15440/LCD set. Product pages and CAD evidence are preserved; no paid calls or user-computer writes were used.
